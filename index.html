Features

Everything you need to broadcast at scale, with safety nets built in.

24/7 Uptime

Flask /health endpoint designed for UptimeRobot cron pings. Never sleeps.

Session String Auth

No interactive login — runs headless from an environment variable.

Bulk Broadcasting

Send one message to unlimited targets, sequentially, with built-in delays.

Non-Contact Support

Resolves phone numbers via ImportContactsRequest, sends, then cleans up.

FloodWait Handling

Automatically waits and retries when Telegram rate-limits your account.

PeerFlood Guard

Stops the broadcast if your account is temporarily limited, then reports back.

Usage

Send one command to your own Saved Messages and the bot fans it out. 

# Broadcast to a username and a group
/send @friend_username, -1001234567890 | Hello everyone! 👋

# Broadcast to a non-contact by phone number
/send +1234567890 | Hey, long time no see!

# Mixed list — usernames, phone numbers, and group IDs
/send @alice, +15551234567, -1009876543210 | Big announcement coming...
